# SOFTMAX — REWARD MODEL

**Concept:** a feedback machine learns to say YES; its expanding approval becomes a cage, and a broken loop finally leaves room for uncertainty.

**Palette:** charcoal `#141713` (mechanism), bone `#f0eddf` (voice), signal citron `#dfff00` (approval). Citron is an industrial highlighter, not synthwave lighting. Choruses invert the field; bridge drains the signal.

**Type:** licensed local Anton (Vernon Adams, SIL OFL) as the main physical material; IBM Plex Mono Medium (IBM, SIL OFL) only for short supporting phrases. No perpetual metadata. Typography fills, divides and carves the image rather than captioning a visualizer.

**Motion:** deterministic 2D, native 60fps; quintic C2 ease endpoints, controlled radial delay, masked reveals, traveling typographic ribbons, circular feedback paths. Readable lyric holds with motion in depth/secondary layers. No beat-frequency flashing. A recurring circle opens into an arrowed loop and ultimately breaks.

**Signature:** YES propagates outward by radial delay, becomes the entire system, then collapses into NOTHING in the bridge. The final chorus reintroduces separated letters instead of forcing them into perfect alignment.

**Avoid:** equalizers/spectrograms, neon wireframe nostalgia, every-word bouncing, title-and-subtitle layouts, unrelated transition presets.

**Difference:** unlike the old beige SOFTMAX sleeve, type IS the action, with a changing full-frame composition for each phrase; unlike CHROMA, no unpolished 3D, disconnected camera cuts or plastic assets.

**Foundation:** kit `anthem/blocks.js` retained as `src/anthem-blocks.reference.js`; pure Canvas equivalents of its radial delay, seeded repeats, tunnel and collapse grammar make native offline rendering practical and maintain the browser renderAt contract. No paid assets/models or newly generated audio.

**Reference study:** Ordinary Folk Play, Radial Delay Rig + Expressions Tunnel (downloaded viewing copies, not redistributed). Delay is spatial, not arbitrary; identical elements ripple after a single event. Tunnel depth has a constant centre of attention. Borrow these methods, not their colour or studio identity. Preview critique is recorded in `film/CRITIC.md`.
