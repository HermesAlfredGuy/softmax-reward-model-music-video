Inspected the real adjacent states in `qa/reference-radial.png` and `qa/reference-tunnel.png`, not merely thumbnails. The radial rig drives repeated cells from distance to one control point: one event propagates instead of random individual bouncing. Tunnel uses nested large shapes and a persistent focal centre, with overlap providing apparent depth. Both techniques are useful in 2D without expensive shaders or a native AE project. Viewing copies are not redistributed or treated as a blanket commercial asset licence.

Translation: the chorus opens its circle through a spatial mask; the bridge's YES grid propagates by Euclidean-distance delay and contracts through the same centre. Type remains the subject. Palette is a bespoke industrial approval signal (citron/charcoal/bone), unrelated to the reference's monochrome demo identity.

The anthem kit was inspected (`BREAKDOWN.md`, `blocks.js`). Retained ideas: one argument, meaningful match continuity, large visual change, title resolve. Not retained: its narrated promo structure, collage stickers or mixed unrelated effect blocks.
